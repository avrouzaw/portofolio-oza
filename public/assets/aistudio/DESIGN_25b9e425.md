## Brand & Style

This design system channels an intimate, highly curated aesthetic merging nostalgic stationery craft with executive precision: the **Coquette Office Desk Flatlay & Open Scrapbook Journal**. Designed for creative professionals, writers, and boutique studio leads, the visual world rejects sterile corporate software in favor of physical tactility, stationery warmth, and editorial sophistication.

### Personality & Emotional Resonance
- **Tactile & Cherished:** Interfaces feel touched by hand, layered with textured cardstocks, washi tape tabs, and die-cut ephemera.
- **Editorial Poise:** The play between opulent editorial serifs and functional typography provides structure, ensuring warmth never compromises workflow density.
- **Thoughtful Whimsy:** Delicate coquette cues—soft ribbon motifs, brass paperclips, and handwritten margins—infuse joy and emotional attachment into everyday digital productivity.

### Design Movement & Aesthetic Framework
The style is **Tactile / Skeuomorphic Realism** blended with **Editorial Minimalism**. Rather than heavy 2010s bevels, it relies on modern paper-layer physics: subtle physical drops, natural paper grain, edge fray, crisp die-cut borders, and warm ambient desk lighting casting soft, directional shadows.

## Colors

The palette balances warm organic substrates with delicate blush pigments and deep carbon-ink legibility.

### Palette Architecture
- **Desk Background (`#F9F8F6`):** The foundational mat surface over which all modules, journals, and desk props reside.
- **Stationery Layers:**
  - **Paper White (`#FFFFFF`):** Reserved for foreground notepads, active document sheets, and crisp die-cut sticker borders.
  - **Cream Note (`#F6EFE6`):** Secondary cards, journal margins, and drafting surfaces.
  - **Kraft Folder Base (`#D2C3B5`) & Shadow (`#BFA996`):** Outer structural drawers, container trays, and tabbed file folders.
- **Accent & Expression:**
  - **Blush Pink (`#E8B8C8`):** Primary highlights, active states, bookmark ribbons, and focused tabs.
  - **Soft Blush Tint (`#F5DCE4`):** Passive fills, hover states, and selection fields.
  - **Subtle Pink Gridline (`#F3D1DC`):** Dot matrices, engineering graph rules, and decorative notebook seams.
- **Ink & Typography Hierarchy:**
  - **Rich Ink (`#2E221E`):** Primary reading color for maximum contrast on light and cream paper.
  - **Espresso Dark (`#4A352E`):** Secondary headers, structural borders, and key icons.
  - **Cocoa Brown (`#7A5C52`):** Subtle metadata, caption text, and inactive iconography.

### Contrast & Application Rules
Never place pure `#FFFFFF` directly against `#F9F8F6` without either an ambient paper drop shadow or a thin kraft hairline keyline. Text rendered in `#E8B8C8` is strictly prohibited; blush pink is a tactile accent and surface tone, while ink tones (`#2E221E` and `#4A352E`) anchor all textual information.

## Typography

The typographic system creates an interplay between historic literary romance and clean, contemporary software readability.

### Type Hierarchy & Pairing
- **Primary Editorial (`Playfair Display`):** Reserved for display moments, section covers, sheet titles, and prominent metrics. Brings the literary authority of a bespoke hardbound journal.
- **Productivity & Meta (`Plus Jakarta Sans`):** Anchors all operational controls, data tables, navigation, inputs, and lengthy body copy. Its crisp geometry balances the ornate character of the display serif.
- **Script Accents (Stylistic Overlay):** Where available via platform font loading or decorative SVG marks, use casual cursive accents (`Caveat` for quick marginal notes, checklist ticks, and date stamps; `Alex Brush` for signature flourishes and ribbon seals). Script type is restricted to non-critical editorial moments and must never be applied to forms or interactive buttons.

### Editorial Guidelines
- Use italicized `Playfair Display` sparingly for introductory statements and pull quotes to amplify the scrapbook journal voice.
- Set uppercase labels in `Plus Jakarta Sans` with expanded letter spacing (`+0.04em`) to simulate stamped typewriter tags.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout simulates an open workspace or organized studio desk. It utilizes a **fluid grid anchored by stationery modules** that evoke spread notebooks, kraft file portfolios, and pinning boards.

### Grid & Composition Rules
- **Desktop (1024px+):** 12-column layout with `2rem` margins and `1.5rem` gutters. Content containers mimic physical paper sheets (`Cream Note` or `Paper White`) positioned over the neutral `#F9F8F6` desk plane.
- **Tablet (768px - 1023px):** 8-column layout with `1.5rem` outer margins. Multi-column desk spreads collapse into single open-journal spreads or vertically stacked dossiers.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column layout with `1rem` margins and `1rem` gutters. Sheets convert into full-bleed card stacks with exposed file-folder tabs along the top or bottom bar.

### Spacing Cadence
Component padding scales around a strict `0.5rem` unit. Use `space-xs` and `space-sm` for tag badges, metadata locks, and micro-interactions; utilize `space-lg` and `space-xl` internally within paper cards to preserve the open, unhurried air of fine print literature.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual depth avoids cold programmatic drop shadows, using directional warm shadows that simulate ambient daylight across an art director's desk.

### The Physical Desk Stacking Order
1. **Layer 0 (Desk Mat):** Ground `#F9F8F6`. Flat, matte, texture-absorbing.
2. **Layer 1 (Kraft Folders & Dossiers):** Deepest physical containers. Casts a soft, wide-spread silhouette: `0 8px 24px -4px rgba(74, 53, 46, 0.08), 0 2px 6px -1px rgba(74, 53, 46, 0.04)`.
3. **Layer 2 (Journal Spreads & Cream Paper Sheets):** Crisp stationery resting atop the folders: `0 4px 14px -2px rgba(46, 34, 30, 0.07), 0 1px 3px 0 rgba(46, 34, 30, 0.04)`.
4. **Layer 3 (Overlays, Cards & Sticky Ephemera):** Floating cards, active dialogs, and peelable memos: `0 16px 32px -6px rgba(46, 34, 30, 0.12), 0 4px 12px -2px rgba(46, 34, 30, 0.06)`.
5. **Layer 4 (Physical Props & Fasteners):** Brass paperclips, washi tape strips, badge clips, and ribbon bookmarks. These project immediate cast shadows directly onto the paper beneath them: `0 2px 4px rgba(46, 34, 30, 0.18)`.

### Tactile Overlays
- **Washi Tape Anchors:** Semi-translucent blush rectangles (`rgba(232, 184, 200, 0.85)`) angled subtly at container corners to visually "affix" cards.
- **Die-Cut Sticker Treatment:** Foreground badges and avatar portraits feature a solid `2px` or `3px` white border (`#FFFFFF`) with a dense, crisp micro-drop shadow (`0 2px 0px rgba(74, 53, 46, 0.12)`).

## Shapes

The geometry reflects cut cardstock, bound notebooks, and hand-sheared ephemera.

### Corner Radius System
- Standard interactive elements (inputs, flat cards, sheet panels) adopt **Soft (`roundedness: 1`)**, matching the subtle curve of guillotine-trimmed paper edges (approx. `4px` / `0.25rem`).
- **File Tabs & Sheet Ears (`rounded-lg`):** Feature rounded top corners (`8px` / `0.5rem`) while retaining square bottom edges to seamlessly merge with underlying folders.
- **Pill Accents:** Reserved exclusively for circular ribbon wax seals, metallic eyelets, badge lanyard loops, and oval stamp badges.
- **Washi Tape & Sticky Strips:** Pure right angles (`0px`) with optional serrated or irregular edge masks along the vertical boundaries.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary (Wax Seal / Ribbon Pink):** Background `#E8B8C8`, text `#2E221E`, crisp `#4A352E` micro-border (`1px`). Hover shifts to `#F5DCE4` with slight `-1px` vertical lift.
- **Secondary (Kraft Card):** Background `#D2C3B5`, text `#2E221E`, subtle inset paper shadow.
- **Tertiary (Desk Outline):** Transparent fill, `1px` border in `#BFA996`, text `#4A352E`.
- **Ephemera Button:** Styled as a die-cut label with a `2px` `#FFFFFF` outline and ambient drop shadow.

### Chips & Badges
- **Status Tags:** Rendered as mini manila/kraft tags or ribbon loops. Text set in `label-sm` with `letter-spacing: 0.04em`.
- **Die-Cut Badges:** Rounded organic shapes encased in an opaque `#FFFFFF` outline with soft drop-shadowing.

### Cards & Dossiers
- **Open Journal Spread:** Two-column card structure backed by `#F6EFE6` with an inner spine shadow running down the center gutter. Faint pink gridlines (`#F3D1DC`) or ruled lines optional for dashboard widget zones.
- **Kraft Dossier:** Outer folder in `#D2C3B5` with an offset file-tab header holding project metadata.

### Form Inputs
- **Field Canvas:** `#FFFFFF` or `#F6EFE6` base with a single crisp bottom rule in `#7A5C52` (lined notebook style) or a complete boxed border in `#D2C3B5`.
- **Focus State:** Border shifts to `#E8B8C8` accompanied by a soft blush glow ring (`0 0 0 3px #F5DCE4`).
- **Placeholder:** Styled in muted `#7A5C52` with an informal, warm character.

### Checkboxes & Radios
- Checkboxes mimic hand-drawn ink squares; active states display an espresso tick mark resembling a pen stroke.
- Radio buttons feature a delicate brass or blush ring with a centered espresso ink pip when selected.

### Scrapbook Accents & Flatlay Props
- **Paperclips:** Metallic wire graphics in brass or rose-gold pinning modal dialogs or secondary reference cards to primary screens.
- **Employee Badge Lanyard:** Sticky sidebars or floating profile widgets can adopt a lanyard sleeve silhouette with a grommet punch hole and twill ribbon anchor.
